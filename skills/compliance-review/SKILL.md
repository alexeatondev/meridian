# Skill: compliance-review

Checklist per run: (1) regime packs in force and their obligations; (2) every hard rule PASS; (3) warnings acknowledged by approvers;
(4) attestations pending and who must sign; (5) ledger integrity; (6) data provenance — synthetic or licensed, license reviewed for AI use;
(7) marketing restrictions on hypothetical performance; (8) required human roles filled before production; (9) retention period applied.
Language: factual, cite rule IDs and IPS sections, never state that something is "compliant" without a human sign-off.
